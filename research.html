Research

Geometry and structure in quantum field theory.

My work connects scattering amplitudes, positive geometries, and mathematical structures with physical questions in gauge theory and cosmology.

01

Scattering amplitudes & positive geometries

Positive geometries provide a way to organize amplitudes and their singularity structures. I have worked on amplituhedra, all-loop ABJM amplitudes, and geometric formulations of amplitude data.

Related publications

All-loop four-point ABJM amplitudesEmergent unitarityMomentum amplituhedron
02

Cosmological wavefunctions

Cosmological observables lead to differential equations and special functions with rich algebraic structure. Recent work explores recursive solutions, cluster adjacency, and quadrangular polylogarithms.

Related publications

de Sitter wavefunction from quadrangular polylogarithmsGeneralised cluster adjacencyDifferential equations and recursive solutions
03

Correlators, f-graphs & graphical bootstrap

Graphical representations make perturbative correlators and amplitudes amenable to systematic study. This direction includes energy correlators, graphical bootstrap methods, and loop-level graph data.

Related publications

Worldsheet methods & color-kinematics

Worldsheet-inspired constructions offer compact ways to study loop integrands, quadratic propagators, and algebraic relations behind color-kinematics duality.
